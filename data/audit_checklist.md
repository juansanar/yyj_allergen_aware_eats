### Instructions for Auditor:
1. Click the **Website** link or the **Google/Yelp Search** links to find the online menu.
2. Audit the menu for the 5-item checklist: Symbols, Statement, Separate Menu, separate Chart.
3. Update the corresponding entries in `data/cache_scraped_data.json` with the real attributes and raw `menu_text`.
4. Re-run `npx tsx src/db/seed.ts` to automatically re-fit the GEE regressions and update the paper & web app.
